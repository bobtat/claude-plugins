## pm-41628428

Saved text: `fulltext/pm-41628428.txt`

**Screener A:** exclude E2: Not randomized

Rationale: Methods, Study and Trial Designs: participants were initially randomly allocated but "requested to change their assigned allocation, and these requests were accommodated to minimize participant dropout"; analysis was per protocol by group actually joined; the paper calls itself quasi-experimental.

Doubt: I2: allocation was random but 9-10% of participants per group switched by preference before the start; the criterion does not say how much departure from random allocation still counts as randomized.

**Screener B:** include 

Rationale: Methods: Sport Motivation Scale (SMT) meets I5; Table 3 gives pre/post means for "EBTT+OCTT" and "OCTT×2" (exergame PS3 Move Virtual Tennis plus on-court vs on-court only; 12 weeks; published 2026). Allocation: "participants were initially randomly allocated" but 7 swapped on request.

Doubt: I2: Titled quasi-experimental; allocation was random but about 10% of participants (7) changed group by request before the start. Does this count as random allocation or as allocation by participant preference?
