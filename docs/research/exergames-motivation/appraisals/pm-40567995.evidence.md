## Coding notes
- `design` = parallel rct: "randomly assigned to either the experimental group ... or the control group ... in a 1:1 ratio" (§2.3); the paper calls itself an "assessor-blinded experimental design" and never says "parallel".
- `trial_scale` = not stated: the paper does not call itself a pilot or feasibility study; the sample size is justified by G*power (§2.6).
- `health_status` = other: participants were recruited as pre-frail (SOF scale), "meeting the “pre-frailty” criteria based on the SOF frailty scale" (§2.2); no list value covers it.
- `setting` = not stated: §2.4 says the control group did the dance "at home" but gives no place for the Kinect sessions. The Discussion says the system lets participants "perform aerobic exercises at home" and §5 says "the exercise environments of the control and experimental groups were different" without saying how.
- `exergame_platform` = console or camera motion game: Microsoft Kinect sensor with software the authors developed ("we developed an aerobic dance training program using the Kinect system"), custom software on a commercial camera.
- `comparator_type` = conventional exercise: control "received aerobic dance exercise program with video at home" (Abstract); video of a dancer, no skeleton or score (§2.4).
- `motivation_instrument` = breq: "Behavior Regulation in Exercise Questionnaire-2 (BREQ-2)" (§2.5).
- `motivation_role` = secondary: §3.2 calls WHOQOL-BREF and BREQ-2 "secondary outcome measures (subjective measures)", while §2.5 names physical fitness components as "The primary outcome measures".
- `direction` = no between-group difference: coded from the reported test, not from the conclusion. Table 2's BREQ-2 row has four P values for the experimental group and two for the control group. Which pair is the between-group test is inferred, not labelled: the footnote says "a Intergroup analysis", "b Intragroup analysis", but the column order and the text show the first two values on the EG row are within-group and the last two between-group (30CST: EG "<0.001 c <0.001 c 0.002 0.277" with text "significant difference between the two groups at 0–8 weeks ( p < 0.002)"; 6MWT "0.104 0.016" with text "significant difference between groups at 0–12 weeks ( p < 0.016)"; WHOQOL-BREF "0.024 0.038" with text "between groups at 0–8 weeks ( p < 0.024) and 0–12 weeks ( p < 0.038)"). On that reading BREQ-2 between-group P is 0.936 (0–8) and 1.000 (0–12). No composite or subscale between-group test is reported. The screening doubt (I6) about the lack of a clearly labelled between-group test is the same issue.
- `followup_result`: the 0–12 week column is the 1-month follow-up (§2.4, §5).

## Domain evidence

### D1 Randomization process
- Sequence and block: "Block randomization with a block size of 4 was used. The allocation was carried out by independent researchers using a computer-generated random number sequence." (§2.3)
- Timing: "After baseline measurements, participants were randomly assigned" (§2.3).
- Concealment: no statement that allocation was concealed until assignment (searched §2.3, §2.4, §3.1). The independent researchers carried out the allocation; the paper does not say how assignments were conveyed.
- Baseline: "No significant differences of the basic characteristics or baseline measurements were observed between groups." (§3.1). Table 1 shows no baseline test for BREQ-2. Table 2 baseline BREQ-2: "EG 47.57 ± 6.75 49.87 ± 8.62 51.27 ± 5.56 0.180 0.008 0.936 1.000" and "CG 53.97 ± 17.22 55.97 ± 14.77 57.67 ± 14.09 0.560 0.279", i.e. 47.57 vs 53.97 at week 0, SDs 6.75 vs 17.22. No test of the baseline difference is shown.
- Numbers: "Initially 65 older adults participated in the study and randomly assigned" but the Abstract says "Sixty participants enrolled" and "randomly assigned into an experimental group ( n = 30) and control group ( n = 30)" (§3.1, Abstract).
- Registration: NCT06216236. Registration date not stated.

### D2 Deviations from intended interventions
- Participants and instructors: "Although the instructors supervising the exercise sessions were aware of group assignments, outcome assessors and participants in both groups remained blinded throughout the study." (§2.3). The paper does not say how participants could be blind to a Kinect system with scoring versus a plain video.
- Analysis population: "But finally 60 participants finished the training session and all the measurements." (§3.1). No statement of intention-to-treat or per-protocol; Table 2 analyses 30 per group. Generalized estimating equation used (§2.6).
- Context differences: "the exercise environments of the control and experimental groups were different, which might have affected participants' motivations." (§5). Control group exercised "at home" with a video (§2.4); where the experimental group trained is not stated.
- Co-interventions, extra exercise or contamination: none reported (searched §2.4, §3, §5). No adherence or attendance data reported.
- Not a crossover trial.

### D3 Missing outcome data
- "Initially 65 older adults participated in the study and randomly assigned to the experimental group or control group. But finally 60 participants finished the training session and all the measurements." (§3.1). Five were lost; the arm they came from, and reasons, are not given in the saved text (Figure 1 content not in the saved text).
- Table 2 and Table 1 report N = 30 per group; no analysis with the missing 5 is described.
- Abstract and §2.4 give n = 30 per group as randomized ("experimental group ( n = 30) and control group ( n = 30)"), which differs from 65 in §3.1.

### D4 Measurement of the outcome
- Instrument: "Behavior Regulation in Exercise Questionnaire-2 (BREQ-2) ( 20 )" and "The Chinese version of the Exercise Behavior Regulation Questionnaire is a measurement tool used to measure the general public's exercise motivation." (§2.5, §2.5.4). Reference 22 is a Chinese translation validated in university students (references list: "Chinese-translated behavioral regulation in exercise questionnaire-2: evidence from university students in the Mainland and Hong Kong of China").
- Reliability: "Cronbach's scale ranged from 0.71 to 0.91 with good internal consistency."
- Administration: "The questionnaire was filled by the subjects themselves before the walking test." (§2.5.4); same description of timing for all arms; "Assessment was performed at three time points: before the training period, right after the training period, and 1 month after." (§2.5). No separate administration description by arm.
- Items: not stated whether items were changed. The five aspects are listed as "internal regulation aspect, identity regulation aspect, interjective regulation aspect, external regulation aspect, and motivation". Table 2 reports a single BREQ-2 value per arm (e.g. 47.57 ± 6.75); how it was formed (sum, mean, relative autonomy index) is not stated, nor the number of items.
- Blinding: participants "remained blinded throughout the study" (§2.3), outcome assessors blinded (Abstract). Whether participants knew the study hypothesis is not stated.

### D5 Selection of the reported result
- Registration: "The trial was registered at http://www.ClinicalTrials.gov ( NCT06216236 )." (§2.1). The registry record was not read; the date, outcomes and time points it lists are not in the saved text. The Abstract's registration link is to "https://register.clinicaltrials.gov/prs/beta/records".
- Protocol: none cited.
- Reported versus administered: §2.5.4 lists five aspects of BREQ-2; Table 2 and §3.2 give only one BREQ-2 score; no subscale results anywhere (searched §3, Table 2, Discussion).
- Time points: BREQ-2 reported at weeks 0, 8, 12 (Table 2), matching the three assessment time points in §2.5.
- Analysis: the GEE "to examine the effect between groups and times" (§2.6); the text of §3.2 reports BREQ-2 as "significant difference in BREQ-2 scores in the experimental group at 0–12 weeks ( p < 0.008), but no changes in the control group", with no between-group BREQ-2 test described in text.
- Sample size was calculated (§2.6) without naming an outcome.

## Inside the paper
- Abstract result: "A significant difference in Behavior Regulation in Exercise Questionnaire-2 (BREQ-2) scores was also noted in the experimental group at 0–12 weeks ( p < 0.008)". Table 2 BREQ-2 row "EG 47.57 ± 6.75 49.87 ± 8.62 51.27 ± 5.56 0.180 0.008 0.936 1.000" shows 0.008 as the within-group test; the between-group values read as 0.936 and 1.000 and the control group moved in the same direction ("CG 53.97 ± 17.22 55.97 ± 14.77 57.67 ± 14.09 0.560 0.279").
- Abstract conclusion "facilitate exercise behavior regulation and improve fitness enthusiasm" and the Conclusion "they show significant improvement in quality of life, exercise behavior regulation and fitness enthusiasm. Thus, aerobic dance exercise using Kinect system was more effective than traditional training" rest on within-group tests for BREQ-2; the between-group BREQ-2 values in Table 2 are 0.936 and 1.000.
- Table 2 footnote "a Intergroup analysis" / "b Intragroup analysis" does not match the position of the values (the first two values on each EG row correspond to within-group tests per §3.2; see Coding notes).
- Table 2 header labels "Week 0–12 P a" in the sixth position, and only the EG row carries four P values while the CG row carries two.
- Number randomized: "Sixty participants enrolled" (Abstract) against "Initially 65 older adults participated in the study and randomly assigned" (§3.1).
- §3.1 "No significant differences of the basic characteristics or baseline measurements were observed between groups", while Table 2 baseline BREQ-2 is 47.57 ± 6.75 (EG) against 53.97 ± 17.22 (CG).
- §3.2 "the experimental group showed improvement on the WHOQOL-BREF during weeks 0–8 ( p < 0.001) and 0–12 ( p < 0.003)"; Table 2 row for WHOQOL-BREF gives "<0.001 c 0.003 c".
- §3.2 "Both the experimental and control groups showed improvement during weeks 0–8 ( p < 0.001)" for 30CST; Table 2 CG 0–8 value is 0.038.
- §3.2 "no changes" for the control group on WHOQOL-BREF, and the Discussion says "both the experimental group and control group showed significant improvements in physical fitness and quality of life"; Table 2 CG WHOQOL-BREF values "0.180 0.586".
- Grip strength Table 2 CG left hand: "21.07 ± 7.60 21.47 ± 7.31 21.47 ± 7.31" (identical week 8 and week 12 mean and SD).
- Discussion of WHOQOL-BREF: §2.5.3 describes the questionnaire as scored with domain scores "from 4 to 20", while Table 2 reports values near 3.6–3.9.
- Participants' blinding: "participants in both groups remained blinded" (§2.3) against an intervention in which the experimental group saw a skeleton and score and the control "did not show the skeletal simulation" (§2.4).
- Funding statement names Kaohsiung Veterans General Hospital as funder and the authors' affiliations include that hospital.
