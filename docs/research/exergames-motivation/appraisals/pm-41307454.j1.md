# Judgment — pm-41307454

- Records: pm-41307454
- Eligibility: clear

## Eligibility
I1: participants pedal a VirZoom bike to play games with goals ("Participants aimed to meet various performance goals at each gate", Intervention Group), which is the protocol's stationary-bike-driving-a-game case. I2: "Randomization was executed using a computer-generated sequence in Microsoft Excel" (Randomization). I3: no-intervention control. I4: "exercising for 1 hour per session, twice weekly, for 4 weeks" (Intervention Group). I5/I6: BREQ-2, all five subscales by arm at baseline and 4 weeks (Table 2). I7: published 15 December 2025; data were collected 2020-2021, but the paper gives no sign of an earlier results report. I8: English. Every criterion is met.

## Reasons
**D1 Some concerns.** The sequence method is named (computer-generated in Excel) and baseline BREQ-2 subscales are similar (Table 1, P = .17 to .81). But nothing is said about allocation concealment, or about who generated the sequence and who assigned participants. The protocol's Some concerns anchor covers exactly that gap. Nothing shows allocation was predictable, so High does not apply.

**D2 Some concerns.** The analysis was of completers (36 of 38). The paper never says intention-to-treat, and it allows outliers to be excluded by a regression procedure without saying how many or from which outcomes. That fails the Low anchor, which allows only exclusions for missing outcome data. The F(1,34) degrees of freedom are consistent with all 36 completers in the motivation analysis, so no exclusion that could affect the result is shown, and High does not apply. Controls were told not to join other PA interventions, but "We did not formally measure the PA levels of the control group throughout the study." (Control Group). This is an unmonitored possible deviation, not a reported one.

**D3 High.** 38 were randomized 1:1 and both dropouts came from the intervention arm. With 19 controls analysed, the arms were 19 and 19, though the paper does not print per-arm randomized numbers. That makes missingness 2/19 (10.5%) in the intervention arm and 0/19 in the control arm. The intervention arm fails the 90% threshold for Low, and the 10.5-point gap between arms exceeds the High anchor's 10 points. The dropouts' reason (concern about an in-person study during COVID-19) is tied to the in-person sessions only that arm attended. No sensitivity or robust analysis is reported. The rating is narrowly over the threshold, and it depends on per-arm figures that follow from the stated totals.

**D4 Some concerns.** BREQ-2 is a published, validated instrument, and nothing suggests its items were modified. "Both groups received identical assessments at baseline and after intervention" (Control Group). That meets the Some concerns anchor. Low is not reachable: participants knew their arm, and nothing says they were unaware of the hypothesis. No High condition applies.

**D5 Some concerns.** The trial cites a ClinicalTrials.gov registration but gives no registration date and does not say what outcomes it names. Recruitment ran August 2020 to March 2021, and nothing shows the registration came before enrolment, so Low is not met. All five subscales and both time points named in the Methods are reported in Table 2, so the High anchor (incomplete reporting, or a registered outcome that differs) is not shown from the paper.
