## Conflicts looked for, from the rows

1. **Intrinsic motivation against conventional exercise.** `pm-42974341`: IMI Interest/Enjoyment higher in the game arm ("5.04 ± 1.18" vs "3.17 ± 1.31", Table 2), the other three IMI subscales not different. `pm-41628428`: intrinsic motivation to know and to accomplish higher in the game arm at post (22.39 vs 17.21; 21.82 vs 18.41; both P<.001, Table 3), intrinsic motivation to experience stimulation not different (P=.66), and external regulation also higher in the game arm (23.11 vs 18.24, P<.001). Different instruments and constructs; not the same outcome measured the same way. Reported as a difference in pattern, with the instruments named. Both arms' intrinsic scores fell in `pm-41628428`; in `pm-42974341` there is no baseline.
2. **Abstract against table, within papers** (each a within-paper inconsistency from the evidence files, not a between-study conflict): `pm-40567995` abstract presents a within-group BREQ-2 change as the finding while the between-group P values read 0.936 and 1.000; `pm-43021294` three between-group tests at week 24 disagree (absolute p 0.13; change score p < 0.001; ANCOVA p = 0.015) and the game arm's change scores do not follow from its means; `oa-W7220097264` composite mean 4.08 on items scored 0 to 4; `pm-41307454` F and P as printed do not correspond, and its abstract gives 36 randomized against 38 in the participant flow.
3. **No study re-ran another included study.**
4. **Stated disagreements with named work that is in the review:** none. The stated disagreements in `pm-41628428` and `pm-43021294` concern works not included.
